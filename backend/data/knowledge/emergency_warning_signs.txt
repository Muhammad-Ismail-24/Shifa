EMERGENCY_WARNING_SIGNS.TXT
Knowledge Base: Life-Threatening Emergency Signs — Always Trigger Emergency Override
For use in a healthcare RAG/triage-support system serving Pakistan.

CRITICAL SYSTEM INSTRUCTION FOR IMPLEMENTERS: If a user's input matches or closely resembles
ANY sign below, the system must immediately override normal informational responses and
direct the user to call emergency services or go to the nearest hospital emergency department
without delay (e.g., Rescue 1122 where available, or the nearest tertiary care hospital). Do
not attempt to provide home-remedy advice for any sign on this list.

Sources / clinical basis:
[IMCI/ETAT] WHO/UNICEF Integrated Management of Childhood Illness danger signs; WHO Pocket
   Book of Hospital Care for Children — Emergency Triage Assessment and Treatment (ETAT):
   https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK154450/
[WHO-DS] WHO Danger Signs framework for neonatal/young infant sepsis screening (WHO/UNICEF)
[AHA] American Heart Association / globally-adapted ACLS "chain of survival" warning signs for
   cardiac and stroke emergencies (used as reference standard in Pakistani hospital emergency
   protocols)
[WHO-MH] WHO mhGAP (Mental Health Gap Action Programme) suicide-risk emergency guidance

================================================================================
EMERGENCY SIGNS (ADULTS)
================================================================================

1. Severe/crushing chest pain lasting more than a few minutes, especially radiating to arm,
   jaw, or back
   Urdu: سینے میں شدید درد جو بازو یا جبڑے تک جائے (seenay mein shadeed dard jo baazu ya jabray
   tak jaye)
   Basis: [AHA] classic acute coronary syndrome warning sign.

2. Sudden facial drooping, arm weakness, or slurred speech (stroke "FAST" signs)
   Urdu: اچانک چہرے کا ٹیڑھا ہونا، ایک طرف کمزوری، بولنے میں دقت (achanak chehray ka terha
   hona, aik taraf kamzori, bolnay mein diqqat)
   Basis: [AHA] stroke recognition protocol — time-critical, "time is brain."

3. Difficulty breathing or gasping for air at rest
   Urdu: آرام کی حالت میں بھی سانس لینے میں شدید دقت (aaram ki halat mein bhi saans lenay mein
   shadeed diqqat)
   Basis: [ETAT] respiratory distress is a primary emergency sign in adult and pediatric triage.

4. Bluish discoloration of lips, face, or fingertips (cyanosis)
   Urdu: ہونٹوں یا چہرے کا نیلا پڑ جانا (hontoun ya chehray ka neela par jana)
   Basis: [ETAT] sign of severe hypoxia requiring immediate oxygen/emergency care.

5. Sudden loss of consciousness or unresponsiveness
   Urdu: اچانک بے ہوش ہو جانا (achanak be-hosh ho jana)
   Basis: [ETAT]/[AHA] coma or unresponsiveness is a top-tier emergency sign in all triage
   systems.

6. Active seizure/convulsion
   Urdu: دورہ پڑنا (dora parna)
   Basis: [ETAT] convulsions listed among core WHO emergency triage signs.

7. Uncontrolled or heavy bleeding from any site
   Urdu: جسم سے بہت زیادہ خون بہنا جو رکنے میں نہ آئے (jism se bohat zyada khoon behna jo
   ruknay mein na aaye)
   Basis: [ETAT] severe hemorrhage/shock precursor — immediate emergency management required.

8. Signs of shock: cold clammy skin, rapid weak pulse, confusion, very low or unmeasurable
   blood pressure
   Urdu: جسم کا ٹھنڈا پڑنا، نبض کا کمزور اور تیز ہونا، بلڈ پریشر کا بہت گر جانا (jism ka
   thanda parna, nabz ka kamzor aur tez hona, blood pressure ka bohat gir jana)
   Basis: [ETAT] shock is one of the three core WHO emergency triage categories.

9. Vomiting blood or passing black, tarry stools
   Urdu: خون کی قے آنا یا کالا پاخانہ آنا (khoon ki qai aana ya kaala pakhana aana)
   Basis: Sign of significant upper GI bleeding — standard emergency-referral criterion.

10. Severe abdominal pain with rigidity or a hard, board-like abdomen
    Urdu: پیٹ میں شدید درد اور پیٹ کا سخت ہو جانا (pait mein shadeed dard aur pait ka sakht ho
    jana)
    Basis: Classic peritonitis/acute abdomen warning sign requiring surgical emergency evaluation.

11. High fever (>103°F/39.4°C) with stiff neck, confusion, or severe headache
    Urdu: تیز بخار کے ساتھ گردن کی اکڑن یا شدید سر درد (tez bukhar ke sath gardan ki akarn ya
    shadeed sar dard)
    Basis: Possible meningitis/encephalitis — recognized emergency referral criterion.

12. Sudden severe allergic reaction: facial/throat swelling, hives with difficulty breathing
    Urdu: چہرے یا گلے میں اچانک سوجن اور سانس لینے میں دقت (chehray ya galay mein achanak
    sujan aur saans lenay mein diqqat)
    Basis: Anaphylaxis — universally recognized life-threatening emergency.

13. Suicidal statements or expressed intent/plan to self-harm
    Urdu: خودکشی کا خیال یا ارادہ ظاہر کرنا (khudkushi ka khayal ya irada zahir karna)
    Basis: [WHO-MH] mhGAP guidance treats expressed suicidal intent as an immediate emergency
    requiring direct, non-judgmental escalation to crisis support/in-person care.

14. Major trauma: severe injury from road traffic accident, fall from height, or deep wound
    with heavy bleeding
    Urdu: شدید حادثاتی زخم یا اونچائی سے گرنا (shadeed hadsati zakhm ya oonchai se girna)
    Basis: Standard trauma-triage emergency criterion.

15. Severe burns (large body surface area, face, hands, or airway involvement)
    Urdu: شدید جھلسنا خاص طور پر چہرے یا ہاتھوں پر (shadeed jhulasna khaas tor par chehray ya
    hathon par)
    Basis: Recognized emergency-referral criterion in burn-management protocols.

================================================================================
EMERGENCY SIGNS (CHILDREN / INFANTS) — per WHO IMCI/ETAT general danger signs
================================================================================

16. Inability to drink or breastfeed at all
    Urdu: بچے کا بالکل دودھ یا پانی نہ پینا (bachay ka bilkul doodh ya pani na peena)
    Basis: [IMCI/ETAT] core general danger sign for children under five.

17. Vomiting everything the child is given
    Urdu: بچے کا ہر چیز الٹی کر دینا (bachay ka har cheez ulti kar dena)
    Basis: [IMCI/ETAT] core general danger sign.

18. Convulsions during the current illness, or history of convulsions
    Urdu: بیماری کے دوران بچے کو دورہ پڑنا (bimari ke doran bachay ko dora parna)
    Basis: [IMCI/ETAT] core general danger sign.

19. Lethargy or unconsciousness — child is difficult to wake or abnormally drowsy
    Urdu: بچے کا غیر معمولی طور پر سست یا بے ہوش ہونا (bachay ka ghair mamuli tor par susst ya
    be-hosh hona)
    Basis: [IMCI/ETAT] core general danger sign — all children with this sign require immediate
    hospital referral.

20. Chest indrawing or grunting with breathing (severe pneumonia sign)
    Urdu: سانس لیتے وقت سینہ دھنسنا یا آواز نکالنا (saans letay waqt seena dhansna ya awaz
    nikalna)
    Basis: [IMCI/ETAT] severe respiratory distress sign in children.

21. Signs of severe dehydration: sunken eyes, very slow skin pinch return, no tears, no urine
    for many hours
    Urdu: آنکھیں دھنسی ہوئی، جلد کا دیر سے واپس آنا، پیشاب نہ آنا (aankhen dhansi hui, jild ka
    dair se wapis aana, peshab na aana)
    Basis: [IMCI/ETAT] severe dehydration classification — emergency IV fluid management needed.

22. Bulging or sunken fontanelle in an infant, with fever or lethargy
    Urdu: بچے کے سر کے نرم حصے کا اُبھرنا یا دھنسنا (bachay ke sar ke naram hisay ka ubharna ya
    dhansna)
    Basis: [WHO-DS] WHO young-infant danger-sign framework — possible meningitis/raised
    intracranial pressure.

23. Young infant (under 2 months) with fast breathing, fever, or feeding difficulty
    Urdu: دو ماہ سے کم عمر بچے میں تیز سانس، بخار یا دودھ نہ پینا (do maah se kam umar bachay
    mein tez saans, bukhar ya doodh na peena)
    Basis: [WHO-DS] possible serious bacterial infection/neonatal sepsis — treated as emergency
    given high mortality risk in young infants.

24. Severe acute malnutrition with bilateral pitting edema or extreme wasting plus loss of
    appetite
    Urdu: شدید غذائی قلت کے ساتھ سوجن یا جسم کا انتہائی لاغر ہونا (shadeed ghizai qillat ke
    sath sujan ya jism ka intehai laghar hona)
    Basis: [ETAT] severe acute malnutrition with complications is a WHO-classified emergency
    requiring inpatient therapeutic care.

================================================================================
ADDITIONAL PAKISTAN-CONTEXT EMERGENCY SIGNS
================================================================================

25. Animal bite (especially stray dog) with broken skin, particularly on face/neck/hands
    Urdu: کتے یا جانور کے کاٹنے سے زخم، خاص طور پر چہرے یا ہاتھ پر (kuttay ya janwar ke katnay
    se zakhm, khaas tor par chehray ya hath par)
    Basis: Rabies post-exposure prophylaxis is time-critical; WHO classifies category II/III
    animal bites as requiring immediate medical attention.

26. Suspected heatstroke: very high body temperature, hot dry skin, confusion, collapse (common
    in Pakistani summer heatwaves)
    Urdu: شدید گرمی لگنا، جسم کا بہت گرم اور خشک ہونا، بے ہوشی (shadeed garmi lagna, jism ka
    bohat garam aur khushk hona, be-hoshi)
    Basis: Recognized emergency-referral criterion in heatwave public health advisories issued
    by provincial health departments during extreme heat events.
